How Experience-Led works

Project-based and part-time embedded engagements with clear pre-agreed outcomes, where co-creation of crisp outcomes with clear impact could be the first outcome! I'm built for golf organisations that want clarity on presumed value before making large commitments.

  1. 01
    Frame the outcome and move fast.We start with what we believe “Outcomes” should look like — the from/to change hypotheses. And we derive the Inputs and Outputs (Actions) that we believe will get us there.  I would take on as many of those Actions as possible. And when they inevitably involve others, I will take permissioned responsibility and accountability for orchestrating communications and complexity. 
  2. 02
    Iteratively test that value where possible.What singular or multiple elements of value are we focused on: player development, tournament programming, media production, product management... or some combination? How can we continuously and leanly test to optimise for intended or found opportunities
  3. 03
    Adapt as we learn. Move on impact.Part of the Outcomes is always a distilled "close the loop" retrospective on what we learned and what value was derived, with practical next steps you can act on immediately or place on the shelf for future opportunity: programmes, events, assets, or product decisions.
